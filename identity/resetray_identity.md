# ResetRay Semantic Identity

ResetRayAI is a public semantic imaging documentation initiative focused on AI-readable quantitative imaging terminology and structured CT/DICOM semantics.

---

# Identity Scope

ResetRayAI repositories describe:

- semantic imaging concepts;
- structured technical imaging terminology;
- AI-readable CT/DICOM semantics;
- anonymized imaging concepts;
- interoperability-oriented documentation.

---

# Technical Orientation

The ecosystem focuses on:

- semantic consistency;
- structured quantitative imaging concepts;
- machine-readable terminology;
- public semantic interoperability.

---

# Non-Clinical Boundary

ResetRayAI public repositories are documentation-oriented and are NOT intended for:

- diagnosis;
- disease classification;
- treatment recommendation;
- clinical decision support;
- emergency interpretation.

---

# Public Positioning

ResetRayAI acts as a semantic infrastructure initiative for structured quantitative imaging documentation.
